About Wanquor Strategy

A research-driven multi-asset strategy company.

Wanquor Strategy focuses on market intelligence, derivatives strategy, market analysis, risk management support and decision support across digital assets and precious metals.

Show the framework. Protect the formula.Public materials explain the firm's methodology, market perspective and research process without publishing proprietary signals, model parameters, scoring logic or client-specific settings.
Why We Exist

Information is abundant. Structure is scarce.

The market does not lack information. The harder problem is organizing information, seeing cross-market relationships and translating research into disciplined decision support.

Core Market View

Markets are becoming increasingly integrated.

Digital assets increasingly interact with traditional markets, institutional capital and established risk frameworks. Research therefore benefits from a multi-asset perspective rather than a single-market lens.

Best Fit

  • Experienced market participants who understand derivatives and market risk.
  • Clients who want to understand why an opportunity matters, not only receive a signal.
  • Organizations that value cross-market context, research continuity and risk discipline.
  • Clients who retain control of their own accounts, assets and execution.
  • Long-term professional relationships built around research, strategy and risk support.

Not the Target Client

  • People seeking quick money, guaranteed returns or risk-free directives.
  • Clients with no relevant experience who expect full dependence on an external signal provider.
  • Anyone expecting Wanquor Strategy to hold, manage or control their funds.
  • Clients focused only on short-term return without accepting derivatives risk.
  • Hype-driven relationships that reject risk discipline or independent decision responsibility.

Legal & Disclosures

Clear boundaries matter.

This website model intentionally does not invent regulatory status, legal entities, licenses, jurisdictions or counsel-approved disclosures that are not present in the supplied materials.

Before productionFinal legal language, governing law, regulatory disclosures, privacy details and entity information should be completed with the company's approved legal information.

Public positioning boundary

  • Wanquor Strategy is presented as a strategy and market intelligence company.
  • The website should not imply that it is an exchange, custodian or asset-holding platform.
  • Clients retain control of their own accounts and assets.
  • No guaranteed return, fixed return or risk-free claim should be used.

Protected information

  • Signal-generation logic and proprietary indicator combinations.
  • Algorithm parameters, quantitative models and internal scoring.
  • Entry/exit criteria, position sizing and risk thresholds.
  • Client-specific parameters and proprietary technology architecture.
